Lionsys Solutions offers multiple pathways for federal agencies to access our IT services quickly, compliantly, and efficiently through established contract vehicles.

Why Contract Vehicles Matter

Contract vehicles eliminate lengthy procurement cycles, reduce administrative burden, and give agencies confidence that they are working with pre-vetted, compliant vendors. As a HUBZone-certified, WOSB, and EDWOSB company, Lionsys qualifies for a range of set-aside and small business contracts that help agencies meet their socioeconomic procurement goals.

Small Business Set-Aside

WOSB / EDWOSB Set-Aside Contracts

As a certified Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Economically Disadvantaged Women-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB), Lionsys is eligible for federal set-aside contracts in NAICS codes where WOSB programs apply. Agencies can sole-source or conduct set-aside competitions.

HUBZone Program

SBA HUBZone Certification

Our HUBZone certification enables federal agencies to meet their HUBZone spending goals while accessing our full range of IT services. HUBZone contracts support economic development in historically underutilized business zones.

Open Market

Open Market / Direct Award

For requirements not covered by existing vehicles, Lionsys is available for direct open-market procurement. We work with agencies to ensure all FAR compliance requirements are met for a smooth, compliant award.

Pursuing

GSA Schedule (In Progress)

Lionsys is actively pursuing a GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) contract to further expand procurement options for federal customers. Contact us for our current timeline and to discuss alternative vehicles in the meantime.
