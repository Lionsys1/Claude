Contracting Pathways

Contract Vehicles

Multiple streamlined pathways to engage Lionsys — reducing procurement complexity and accelerating time-to-award.

WOSB / EDWOSB
Active

Women-Owned Set-Aside

As a certified WOSB and EDWOSB, Lionsys qualifies for federal set-aside procurements across applicable NAICS codes. Agencies can conduct set-aside competitions or issue sole-source awards within program thresholds — helping meet small business and socioeconomic spending goals.

  • Eligible for competitive set-aside procurements
  • Sole-source authority within dollar thresholds
  • Supports agency WOSB/EDWOSB spending goals
HUBZone
Active

SBA HUBZone Program

Our SBA HUBZone certification enables agencies to fulfill mandatory HUBZone spending requirements while accessing Lionsys's full breadth of IT capabilities. HUBZone contracts simultaneously support economic development in historically underutilized communities.

  • Price evaluation preference in full-and-open competition
  • Eligible for HUBZone set-aside and sole-source awards
  • Supports 3% mandatory HUBZone spending goal
GSA MAS
Pursuing

GSA Multiple Award Schedule

Lionsys is actively pursuing a GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) contract to expand federal procurement options and reduce per-award administrative burden for agency partners. Contact us for our current timeline and interim contracting pathways.

  • Streamlined ordering under FAR 8.4
  • Pre-negotiated pricing and terms
  • Contact us for current status and alternatives
Open Market
Available

Open Market / Direct Award

For requirements outside established vehicles, Lionsys is fully available for open-market procurement in accordance with FAR requirements. Our contracting team will work directly with your procurement office to structure a compliant, efficient award process.

  • Full FAR compliance on all engagements
  • Flexible Statement of Work structuring
  • Time & materials, fixed price, and cost-plus arrangements
