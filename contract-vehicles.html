Contract Vehicles

Procurement Made
Simple & Compliant.

Lionsys Solutions provides multiple contracting pathways for federal and state agencies — backed by SBA certifications and a commitment to seamless, compliant procurement.

📋

Why Contract Vehicles Matter

Contract vehicles reduce procurement complexity, accelerate time-to-award, and give agencies confidence they are engaging with pre-vetted, compliant vendors. Our SBA certifications — WOSB, EDWOSB, and HUBZone — make Lionsys an attractive partner for agencies seeking to meet small business and socioeconomic procurement goals while accessing high-quality IT services.

Women-Owned Set-Aside

WOSB / EDWOSB Set-Aside Contracts

As a certified Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Economically Disadvantaged Women-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB), Lionsys qualifies for federal set-aside procurements in applicable NAICS codes. Agencies can conduct set-aside competitions or sole-source awards within program thresholds.

HUBZone Program

SBA HUBZone Certification

Our HUBZone certification helps federal agencies meet mandatory HUBZone spending targets while accessing Lionsys's full range of IT capabilities. HUBZone contracts also support economic development in historically underutilized communities.

Open Market

Open Market / Direct Award

For requirements outside established vehicles, Lionsys is available for direct open-market procurement. We support full FAR compliance and will work with your contracting office to ensure a smooth, efficient award process.

In Progress

GSA MAS Schedule (Pursuing)

Lionsys is actively pursuing a GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) contract to expand federal procurement options. Contact us to discuss current contracting pathways and our expected MAS timeline.
