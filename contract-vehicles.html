Contracting FAQs

Everything government contracting officers and program managers need to know about engaging Lionsys.

Lionsys pursues and holds multiple contract vehicles to simplify the procurement process for government clients. Please contact us directly for the most current list of active vehicles, including any GWACs, BPAs, or agency-specific IDIQs. We are continuously expanding our contract portfolio.

Our primary NAICS codes are 541511 (Custom Computer Programming Services) and 518210 (Data Processing and Hosting). We also operate under 541512, 541513, 541519, 541690, 561320, and 611420 — covering the full range of IT professional services, cybersecurity, cloud hosting, staff augmentation, and training.

HUBZone-certified vendors like Lionsys are eligible for set-aside contracts, sole-source awards up to $4.5M for services, and a 10% price evaluation preference in full-and-open competitions. Contracting officers can search for HUBZone vendors at SAM.gov or contact the SBA for guidance on HUBZone procurement procedures.
