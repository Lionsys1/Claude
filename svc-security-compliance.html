Operationalizing the NIST AI Risk Management Framework

As AI systems become embedded in critical operations, managing AI risk is no longer optional. Lionsys applies the NIST AI RMF — the leading standard for responsible AI governance in U.S. and Canadian organizations — to help clients build trustworthy, auditable, and resilient AI programs from the ground up.

🏛️
Govern
Establish accountability structures, policies, and an organizational culture of responsible AI.
🗺️
Map
Categorize AI systems and identify risks across context, data, models, and operational impact.
📊
Measure
Test, evaluate, verify, and validate AI systems using TEVV methods and measurable performance metrics.
⚙️
Manage
Prioritize, respond to, and continuously monitor AI risks with documented response plans and audit trails.
