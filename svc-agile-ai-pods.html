Overview

From AI Vision to Production in Weeks, Not Months

Most organizations want AI but struggle to move from concept to production. Lionsys AI Team Pods solve this by placing a complete, pre-built engineering team inside your environment.

A Lionsys AI Team Pod is a purpose-built, cross-functional squad of data engineers, ML engineers, DevOps specialists, and product thinkers — deployed into your organization and working in tight 2-week Agile sprints. No long hiring cycles. No ramp-up delays. No misaligned contractors. Just focused, embedded AI engineering that ships working solutions fast and builds your team's capabilities in the process.
